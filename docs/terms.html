1. What Sysight does

Sysight shows information about your Windows PC and lets you end processes, change third-party services, turn startup apps on or off, launch uninstallers and delete cache files. You decide which actions to take.

2. Use with care

  • Ending a process can lose unsaved work in it. This can't be undone.
  • Stopping or disabling a service, or turning off a startup app, can stop the related software or hardware from working until you undo it. Sysight records these changes in Change history so you can reverse them.
  • Cleanup permanently deletes files in the selected cache and temp folders. This can't be undone.
  • Sysight blocks actions on key Windows processes and Microsoft services, but no list is complete. Only change things you understand, and keep backups of important data.
