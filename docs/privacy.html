Sysight Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Applies to Sysight version 1.0 and later for Windows

1. Who we are

Sysight is published by astrosurfr, an individual developer based in India ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, contact us through the issue tracker at github.com/NiksheyYadav/sysight/issues or through the support contact on the Sysight Microsoft Store page.

2. Summary

Sysight runs entirely on your computer. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal data. Sysight has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry, and the app itself makes no network requests.

3. Information Sysight reads on your device

To show you what is running on your PC, Sysight reads information that already exists on your device, only while you use it:

WhatWhy
Running processes, their file paths and publishersProcesses view
Network connections and listening portsNetwork view
Services, kernel drivers and their signaturesServices and Kernel drivers views
Startup entries and installed appsStartup and Installed apps views
Folder and file names and sizesStorage explorer and Cleanup
Computer name, user name, CPU, memory and drive detailsOverview

Some of this, such as your user name or the names of files and folders, can be personal data. It is processed in your computer's memory, shown only to you, and never sent to us or anyone else.

4. Information Sysight saves on your device

  • Change history (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Sysight\changes.json): the services and startup apps you changed, with the time, so you can undo them. It keeps the most recent 500 entries.
  • WebView settings (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Sysight\WebView): data the built-in Microsoft Edge WebView2 component needs to display the app.

These files stay on your computer. Uninstalling Sysight may leave this folder behind; you can delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\Sysight at any time to remove it completely.

5. Third-party components

Sysight uses components of Microsoft Windows, including the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, and is distributed through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft may collect diagnostic or usage data through Windows, WebView2 and the Store according to your Windows privacy settings and the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We do not receive that data.

6. Actions you take

Sysight only changes your system when you click an action: ending a process, changing a service, turning a startup app on or off, launching an uninstaller, or cleaning a cache. Cleanup permanently deletes the files in the selected cache folders. These actions affect only your own computer.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act. If you believe we hold your data, or want to raise a privacy concern, contact us as described in section 1 and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Sysight is a general-purpose system utility. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version at this address with a new effective date. If a future version of Sysight ever collects data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
