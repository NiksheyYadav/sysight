Sysight Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Summary

Sysight does not collect, store, sell or send any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no telemetry, and it makes no network requests of its own.

What Sysight reads on your PC

To do its job, Sysight reads information that is already on your computer: running processes, network connections, services, kernel drivers, startup entries, installed apps, and folder sizes. This information is shown to you on screen and is never transmitted anywhere.

What Sysight saves on your PC

  • A change history file (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Sysight\changes.json) listing service and startup changes you made, so you can undo them.
  • Display settings for the built-in Microsoft Edge WebView2 component in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Sysight\WebView.

Both stay on your computer and are removed when you delete that folder.

Changes you make

Sysight only changes your system when you click an action (end a process, change a service, toggle a startup app, run an uninstaller, or clean a cache), and asks you to confirm the risky ones first.

Children

Sysight does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the Sysight GitHub repository or use the support contact listed on the Microsoft Store page.
